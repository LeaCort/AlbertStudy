Further reading
Everything in this book is standard, and two freely available texts develop it further with more exam-
ples and more depth. Read them alongside these chapters whenever you want a second exposition
or extra practice.
• Jeremy Orloff and Jonathan Bloom, Introduction to Probability and Statistics (MIT OpenCourse-
Ware 18.05, free): the closest match to this course. Its notes on continuous random variables, the
central limit theorem, confidence intervals and significance testing cover exactly our chapters,
with worked problems and solutions.
• Joseph K. Blitzstein and Jessica Hwang, Introduction to Probability (2nd edition, free PDF): a fuller
reference. Chapter 5 (continuous random variables) and Chapter 10 (limit theorems) go deeper
into the mathematics behind the Law of Large Numbers and the Central Limit Theorem.
