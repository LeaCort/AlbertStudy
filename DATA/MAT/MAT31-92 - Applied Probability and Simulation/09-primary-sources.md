Primary sources
The applied, simulation-first spirit of this course draws on a small number of accessible books; each
is the natural next step for a reader who wants more.
• Allen B. Downey, Think Stats: Exploratory Data Analysis (O’Reilly, free online). The closest com-
panion to this course: probability and statistics done in Python through simulation and resampling
rather than calculus. Read it alongside the simulation and resampling chapters.
• David Diez, Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel & Christopher Barr, OpenIntro Statistics (free). A careful con-
ceptual grounding for populations, distributions, chi-square tests and inference — the reference
for the testing chapters.
• Charles Wheelan, Naked Statistics (W. W. Norton, 2013). Non-technical intuition for variability,
sampling, and why an estimate without its uncertainty misleads — the plain-English counterpart
to the discipline this whole course insists on.
