# Further reading

Version of 13 September 2026 Module page
Further reading
• A. Damodaran, Applied Corporate Finance and Investment Valuation — the invest / finance /
return framework, and the cost of capital treated as a chain of judgments rather than a lookup.
• J. Berk & P. DeMarzo, Corporate Finance — a thorough reference for the time value of money,
NPV and IRR, CAPM, WACC, capital structure, and payout policy.
• F. Modigliani & M. Miller (1958), “The Cost of Capital, Corporation Finance and the Theory of
Investment” — the original irrelevance proposition that Chapter 7 builds on.
• W. Sharpe (1964) and J. Lintner (1965) — the papers that introduced the Capital Asset Pricing
Model used in Chapter 4.
