Primary sources and further reading
The frameworks in this book come from a small number of primary works. To go deeper, read the
originals:
• Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton — Getting to Yes. The Harvard Negotiation
Project: BATNA, interests versus positions, and principled, value-creating negotiation (Chapter 1).
• Robert Cialdini — Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion. The six principles of influence
and the evidence behind them (Chapter 2).
• Daniel Kahneman — Thinking, Fast and Slow. Anchoring, framing, and loss aversion, from
the work with Amos Tversky (Chapter 2).
• Neil Rackham — SPIN Selling. The four question types, derived from analysis of thousands of
real sales calls (Chapter 3).
• AIDA is a classic advertising model attributed to E. St. Elmo Lewis in the late nineteenth century;
any marketing-communications text covers it (Section 3.5).
